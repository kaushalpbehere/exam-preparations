ITIL v5 Foundation

LAST-MINUTE CRAM (ITIL v5): Remember the focus on AI integration, automated service management, value streams, digital transformation, and advanced ITSM capabilities.

Set 5 (Questions 201-250)

Q201. What does the term 'Service Consumption' entail? (A) Only paying bills (B) The activities performed by an organization to consume services, including managing consumer resources and receiving/benefiting from the service (C) Buying new hardware (D) Writing software code
Answer: B - Explanation: Service consumption is the active engagement on the part of the user/organization receiving the service.
Q202. What is an 'IT Asset' formally defined as? (A) Everything a company owns (B) Any financially valuable component that can contribute to the delivery of an IT product or service (C) Only physical servers (D) Only software code
Answer: B - Explanation: It encompasses hardware, software, networking, cloud instances, and even specific high-value datasets.
Q203. Which ITIL dimension considers the organizational culture? (A) Information and Technology (B) Partners and Suppliers (C) Organizations and People (D) Value Streams and Processes
Answer: C - Explanation: Implementing Agile or DevOps requires a massive cultural shift (trust, blamelessness, collaboration). This lives squarely in the 'Organizations and People' dimension.
Q204. How does ITIL define 'Cost'? (A) Only the price tag on hardware (B) The amount of money spent on a specific activity or resource (C) Always an exact known number (D) Never related to risk
Answer: B - Explanation: Cost must be evaluated contextually. Removing a service eliminates its direct cost, but might impose new, hidden costs on the business trying to work without it.
Q205. How are 'Cost' and 'Risk' related in service provision? (A) They are completely independent (B) Providers absorb specific costs and risks on behalf of the consumer; however, using a service also introduces new costs (subscription fees) and new risks (dependency on the provider) (C) Providers eliminate all risk (D) Consumers eliminate all cost
Answer: B - Explanation: The fundamental value proposition of a service is shifting the burden of cost/risk from the consumer to the provider, but it is never a 100% transfer.
Q206. 'Capacity and Performance Management' must balance three factors. Which is NOT one of them? (A) Cost (B) Agility (C) Performance (D) Demand
Answer: B - Explanation: The classic triangle for this practice is balancing Cost (buying more servers), Performance (speed/latency), and Demand (how many users need it).
Q207. What does the 'Design and Transition' activity of the SVC transform? (A) Raw materials into code (B) It transforms 'Demand' and 'Expectations' (which were clarified in 'Engage' and 'Plan') into actual, operational 'Products and Services' (C) Incidents into problems (D) Risks into costs
Answer: B - Explanation: This activity takes the blueprint and the business need, and transitions it safely into a live, usable state.
Q208. What is the primary focus of 'Business Analysis' as a practice in ITIL? (A) Financial accounting (B) Analyzing a part of the business, defining its needs, and recommending solutions to address those needs and/or solve a business problem (C) Writing code (D) Managing HR
Answer: B - Explanation: Business Analysts bridge the gap by deeply understanding the business problem before IT tries to throw a technical solution at it.
Q209. What is a 'Sponsor' in ITIL terminology? (A) The person who uses the service daily (B) The person who authorizes the budget for service consumption (C) The IT engineer (D) An external vendor
Answer: B - Explanation: The Sponsor pays for it. The Customer defines the requirements. The User interacts with it. (Often these are the same person, but legally/financially they refer to different roles).
Q210. What is a 'Customer' in ITIL terminology? (A) The person who authorizes the budget (B) The person who defines the requirements for a service and takes responsibility for the outcomes of service consumption (C) The person who fixes the software (D) The IT Helpdesk
Answer: B - Explanation: The customer negotiates the SLA and explicitly defines what 'Value' means for their specific context.
Q211. What is a 'User' in ITIL terminology? (A) The person who pays the bill (B) The person who interacts with and uses the service on a day-to-day basis (C) The software developer (D) The CEO exclusively
Answer: B - Explanation: Users care about usability (UX) and reliability. They are the ones actually invoking the Service Actions.
Q212. What distinguishes 'Products' from 'Services'? (A) They are identical (B) A product is a configuration of an organization's resources designed to offer value. A service is a means of enabling value co-creation by facilitating outcomes. (Products are the building blocks of services) (C) Services are physical; products are digital (D) Products are cheaper
Answer: B - Explanation: An organization creates a software 'product'. They bundle that product with hosting, support, and backups to offer a complete 'service' to a client.
Q213. What is the goal of the 'Engage' activity in the Service Value Chain? (A) To build software (B) To provide a good understanding of stakeholder needs, continual engagement, and transparency, and to foster good relationships with all stakeholders (C) To resolve incidents (D) To track assets
Answer: B - Explanation: Engage acts as the 'front door' to the Service Value Chain for all external interactions (gathering demand, reporting on performance).
Q214. Which guiding principle emphasizes that you cannot 'agile' your way out of a bad foundation? (A) Start where you are (B) Think and work holistically (C) Keep it simple and practical (D) Optimize and automate
Answer: B - Explanation: Holistic thinking ensures that before you speed up the 'Tech' dimension, you ensure the 'Process' and 'People' dimensions can handle the speed.
Q215. The 'Continual Improvement Register' (CIR) should ideally be: (A) Hidden from staff (B) A single, visible, and prioritized database connecting tactical improvements to strategic goals (C) A printed binder (D) Only used by management
Answer: B - Explanation: The CIR fails if it becomes a dumped-in backlog that is never reviewed. It must be actively prioritized.
Q216. How does ITAM (IT Asset Management) assist Information Security Management? (A) By writing firewall rules (B) By providing an accurate inventory of assets; you cannot protect what you do not know you have (C) By arresting hackers (D) ITAM does not assist security
Answer: B - Explanation: Over 50% of security breaches occur on 'shadow IT' or forgotten assets. ITAM's primary security value is sheer visibility.
Q217. In the context of the SVS, what does 'Agility' mean? (A) Writing code fast (B) The ability of an organization to move and adapt quickly, flexibly, and decisively to support internal changes (C) Ignoring processes (D) Working only on small projects
Answer: B - Explanation: Agility is the ability to pivot internally without breaking the system.
Q218. In the context of the SVS, what does 'Resilience' mean? (A) Having duplicate servers (B) The ability of an organization to anticipate, prepare for, respond to, and adapt to both incremental changes and sudden disruptions from an external perspective (C) Never failing (D) Refusing to adopt new tech
Answer: B - Explanation: Resilience is the ability to absorb a shock (like a sudden market shift or a cyberattack) and continue operating.
Q219. What is a typical 'Outcome' of the Service Desk practice? (A) 500 tickets logged (B) Restoring users' ability to work effectively, thereby minimizing business downtime and frustration (C) Buying new headsets (D) Training a new agent
Answer: B - Explanation: Again, focus on the business result. The outcome is not 'we closed the ticket'; the outcome is 'the marketing department can publish their campaign'.
Q220. What is 'Event Management' closely related to in modern cloud architectures? (A) Spreadsheets (B) Observability and automated alerting platforms (C) HR hiring (D) Physical cables
Answer: B - Explanation: Modern 'Event Management' is entirely driven by complex observability tools (like Datadog or splunk) that aggregate thousands of metrics and use AI to decide which 'events' matter.
Q221. Which ITIL practice relies most heavily on the 'Known Error Database' (KEDB)? (A) Service Level Management (B) Problem Management (and Incident Management for applying workarounds) (C) Supplier Management (D) Financial Management
Answer: B - Explanation: Problem Management populates the KEDB. Incident Management uses the KEDB to quickly find workarounds to restore service fast.
Q222. What is the fundamental difference between a 'Standard Change' and a 'Normal Change'? (A) Normal changes are faster (B) Standard changes are pre-authorized due to low, well-understood risk; Normal changes must follow a defined process of assessment and authorization (e.g., CAB) based on their risk (C) Standard changes are only hardware (D) Normal changes never fail
Answer: B - Explanation: Driving as much work as possible into the 'Standard Change' category (via automation) is a primary goal of Agile ITSM to increase velocity.
Q223. What role does automation play in 'Deployment Management' in ITIL v5? (A) It is forbidden (B) It is highly encouraged (e.g., CI/CD pipelines) to standardize the process, eliminate human error (toil), and enable rapid, frequent deployments (C) It only applies to testing (D) It slows things down
Answer: B - Explanation: Manual deployments are slow, error-prone, and stressful. Automated pipelines are the backbone of modern IT service delivery.
Q224. Which practice involves understanding the relationships between different CIs (Configuration Items)? (A) Asset Management (B) Service Configuration Management (C) Release Management (D) Service Desk
Answer: B - Explanation: Service Configuration Management builds the map (CMDB) showing that Server A connects to Switch B to support App C.
Q225. What is the primary reason why 'Problem Management' should NOT be performed by the 'Service Desk'? (A) Service Desk agents are not smart enough (B) They require fundamentally different mindsets and time constraints; Service Desk must restore service instantly (react), while Problem Management must take time to investigate deeply (analyze) (C) It costs too much money (D) Software limits prevent it
Answer: B - Explanation: If you ask a person to both put out the fire (Incident) and investigate who started the fire (Problem), they will always prioritize putting out the fire. Problem management requires dedicated time.
Q226. How does ITIL define 'Value'? (A) A specific dollar amount (B) The perceived benefits, usefulness, and importance of something (C) The cost of hardware (D) The number of features in software
Answer: B - Explanation: Value is completely subjective. A complex piece of software has zero value if the users find it too hard to use.
Q227. What is 'Value Co-creation'? (A) Charging the customer twice (B) An active collaboration between providers and consumers, acknowledging that value is not delivered 'to' a customer, but realized 'with' them as they use the service (C) Hiring consultants (D) Using two software vendors
Answer: B - Explanation: A gym membership (the service provider) delivers zero value unless the customer (the consumer) actually goes to the gym and works out.
Q228. What does 'Focus on Value' mean regarding internal IT projects? (A) They shouldn't happen (B) Even internal infrastructure upgrades must eventually trace back to enabling an outcome that provides value to the external customer or the business bottom line (C) They must generate direct revenue (D) They must be done by Friday
Answer: B - Explanation: If an internal project (like upgrading a database) doesn't make the application faster, more secure, or more reliable for the end-user, why are you doing it? It must link to value.
Q229. 'Progress Iteratively with Feedback' is most closely aligned with which software development methodology? (A) Waterfall (B) Agile/Scrum (C) Spiral (D) V-Model
Answer: B - Explanation: Breaking large initiatives into rapid 'sprints' to validate assumptions and alter course based on feedback is the core of Agile.
Q230. Why is 'Feedback' critical in iterative progress? (A) To complain about management (B) Because complex environments change constantly; a plan made on day 1 is often invalid by day 30, requiring constant course correction (C) To delay the project (D) To satisfy HR requirements
Answer: B - Explanation: In non-linear, unpredictable cloud environments, the only way to succeed is to take small steps, see what happens (feedback), and adjust.
Q231. The 'Organizations and People' dimension stresses the importance of what regarding company culture? (A) Strict dress codes (B) A culture of trust, transparency, and a blame-free environment to encourage psychological safety and innovation (C) Long working hours (D) Siloed teams
Answer: B - Explanation: You cannot implement modern Agile/DevOps practices if people are terrified of being fired for making a mistake. Blame-free cultures encourage finding root causes, not scapegoats.
Q232. What is the impact of 'Shadow IT' according to the 'Information and Technology' dimension? (A) It increases security (B) It creates hidden risks, fragmented data, and compliance nightmares when business units buy SaaS tools without IT's knowledge (C) It saves money (D) It improves network speed
Answer: B - Explanation: While it shows business agility, Shadow IT completely circumvents Governance, Asset Management, and Security practices.
Q233. The 'Partners and Suppliers' dimension is characterized by a continuum stretching between: (A) Cheap and expensive (B) Goods procurement (buying a laptop) to profound strategic partnership (co-managing a massive cloud migration) (C) Internal and external (D) Fast and slow
Answer: B - Explanation: Not all suppliers are equal. Buying pens is a transaction. Hiring AWS for your entire infrastructure is a strategic partnership requiring intense Relationship Management.
Q234. In 'Value Streams and Processes', what is 'Waste'? (A) Trash in the office (B) Any activity that consumes resources (time/money) but creates no value for the customer (C) Failed code (D) Unused servers
Answer: B - Explanation: Lean theory identifies many wastes: waiting time, unnecessary manual motion, over-processing, and defects. Eliminating waste speeds up the value stream.
Q235. What is the difference between a 'Value Stream' and a 'Process'? (A) They are identical (B) A Process is a set of interrelated activities that transform inputs into outputs. A Value Stream traverses multiple processes and departments to deliver an end-to-end outcome to a customer (C) A Value Stream is faster (D) A Process is only for IT
Answer: B - Explanation: For example, 'Employee Onboarding' is a Value Stream. It touches the HR process, the IT provision process, and the Facilities process.
Q236. What is 'Opportunity' in the context of the SVS inputs? (A) A scheduled downtime (B) A change in the market, a new technology, or a new business strategy that IT can leverage to create new value (C) Fixing a bug (D) Hiring a new employee
Answer: B - Explanation: Opportunity is proactive. Demand is reactive (the business asks for something). Opportunity is IT saying 'Hey, we could use AI to do this better'.
Q237. The 'Guiding Principles' of ITIL are described as: (A) Strict laws with penalties (B) Universal and enduring recommendations that guide an organization in all circumstances, regardless of changes in its goals, strategies, type of work, or management structure (C) Only for beginners (D) Temporary suggestions
Answer: B - Explanation: They provide a behavioral compass. When you don't know what process to follow, follow the principles (e.g., Focus on Value, Keep it Simple).
Q238. In the Service Value Chain, what is the role of 'Plan'? (A) Writing code (B) Ensuring a shared understanding of the vision, current status, and improvement direction for all four dimensions across the organization (C) Fixing servers (D) Delivering hardware
Answer: B - Explanation: 'Plan' happens at the strategic, portfolio, and operational levels (e.g., strategic 5-year plans, or planning tomorrow's sprint).
Q239. In the Service Value Chain, what does 'Improve' do? (A) Ensures continual improvement of products, services, and practices across all value chain activities and the four dimensions (B) Only fixes bugs (C) Replaces old hardware (D) Creates marketing materials
Answer: A - Explanation: It's the execution engine for the 'Continual Improvement' practice.
Q240. Which practice is responsible for acknowledging, logging, and classifying issues reported by users? (A) Service Desk (B) Change Enablement (C) Release Management (D) Deployment Management
Answer: A - Explanation: The Service Desk must properly log and classify tickets (as an incident vs a request) so they route to the correct value stream.
Q241. What is the definition of a 'Major Incident'? (A) An incident reported by a CEO (B) An incident with significant business impact requiring an immediate coordinated resolution (C) Any incident that takes over an hour to fix (D) A standard service request
Answer: B - Explanation: Defining what constitutes 'Significant Business Impact' is crucial to ensure Major Incident procedures aren't triggered unnecessarily.
Q242. What is a typical metric for 'Incident Management'? (A) Lines of code written (B) Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR) - the average time taken to fix the issue and restore service (C) Cost of servers (D) Number of software licenses
Answer: B - Explanation: MTTR is a classic IT metric, though in modern ITIL, user satisfaction (CSAT) scores regarding the incident resolution are equally critical.
Q243. How does 'Problem Management' utilize 'Trend Analysis'? (A) By predicting stock market prices (B) By analyzing historical incident data (e.g., seeing that the printer goes offline every Tuesday afternoon) to proactively identify underlying problems before they cause an outage (C) By guessing (D) By asking users
Answer: B - Explanation: Proactive Problem Management spots patterns in the noise to fix structural issues.
Q244. What is the difference between 'Change Enablement' and 'Change Management'? (A) They are different practices (B) 'Change Enablement' is the ITIL v4/v5 name for what was formerly called 'Change Management' in v3, meant to sound less bureaucratic and emphasize enabling speed over just restricting change (C) One is Agile, one is Waterfall (D) There is no difference in spelling
Answer: B - Explanation: The shift in terminology reflects a shift in mindset: the goal is to *enable* safe changes as fast as possible, not to manage and slow them down.
Q245. What is the primary focus of 'Continual Improvement'? (A) Firing people (B) Aligning the organization's practices and services with changing business needs through the ongoing, incremental improvement of products, services, and practices (C) Lowering salaries (D) Buying new tools
Answer: B - Explanation: You are never 'done' improving. As technology and business goals change, IT must continuously adapt.
Q246. What is a 'SWOT Analysis' often used for in Continual Improvement? (A) Debugging code (B) Assessing the Current State by identifying Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats (C) Formatting hard drives (D) Managing payroll
Answer: B - Explanation: It's a standard business tool to understand where an organization currently stands before plotting an improvement trajectory.
Q247. What does the 'Service Catalogue' provide to the 'Service Level Management' practice? (A) A list of bugs (B) A defined list of active services against which Service Level Agreements (SLAs) can be negotiated and measured (C) Financial budget (D) Server specifications
Answer: B - Explanation: You cannot negotiate an SLA for a service if you don't know exactly what that service entails, which is defined in the Catalogue.
Q248. In modern ITSM, how should organizations handle a high volume of repetitive 'Service Requests' (e.g., password resets)? (A) Hire 100 more Service Desk agents (B) Implement self-service portals backed by total automation (Zero-Touch Resolution) (C) Ignore the requests (D) Make the users fill out paper forms
Answer: B - Explanation: 'Optimize and Automate'. This reduces toil, drastically lowers costs, and gives the user an instant result (better UX).
Q249. Which Service Value Chain activity ensures that services are delivered to the users as agreed in the SLA? (A) Plan (B) Engage (C) Obtain/Build (D) Deliver and Support
Answer: D - Explanation: This is the operational end of the chain where Incident Management, Request Management, and Service Desk live to ensure the promised service keeps running.
Q250. Final question: What is the ultimate goal of adopting the ITIL v5 framework? (A) To pass an exam (B) To establish a rigid bureaucracy (C) To create a flexible, holistic operating model that enables the entire organization to co-create continuous, rapidly changing value with its stakeholders (D) To install a specific brand of software
Answer: C - Explanation: ITIL is just a tool. Its only purpose is to help the business leverage technology to win in a complex, fast-moving digital world.
