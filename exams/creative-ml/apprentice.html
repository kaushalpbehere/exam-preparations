The Apprentice (Foundations)

Academic Theory (MSc Level): Linear Algebra & Probability. Focus on Eigenvectors/Eigenvalues for PCA, singular value decomposition (SVD), and Bayesian probability foundational to predictive analytics.
Q1. What is the primary purpose of Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) in predictive modeling? (A) Classification (B) Dimensionality reduction (C) Hyperparameter tuning (D) Activation function
Answer: B - Explanation: SVD factors a matrix into singular vectors and values, which is the mathematical foundation for PCA (Principal Component Analysis), used to reduce dimensionality while preserving variance.
Q2. In Bayesian probability, what represents our updated belief after observing new data? (A) Prior probability (B) Likelihood (C) Posterior probability (D) Marginal probability
Answer: C - Explanation: The posterior probability is the updated probability of a hypothesis given the new evidence, calculated using Bayes' theorem by multiplying the prior by the likelihood and dividing by the marginal.
