Q1. In a decision tree classifier, what does Information Gain measure? (A) The reduction in entropy after a dataset split (B) The number of leaf nodes (C) The error rate of the model (D) The correlation between features
Answer: A - Explanation: Information Gain calculates the difference between the entropy of the parent node and the weighted sum of the entropies of the child nodes, aiming to find the split that maximizes this reduction.
Q2. What is a primary limitation of the K-Means clustering algorithm? (A) It cannot handle large datasets (B) It guarantees finding the global optimum (C) It is sensitive to the initial choice of centroids (D) It only works with categorical data
Answer: C - Explanation: K-Means is a heuristic algorithm that converges to a local optimum. Its final clustering result heavily depends on the random initialization of the centroids, often requiring multiple runs with different seeds.
